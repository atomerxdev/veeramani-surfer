About Veeramani Surfer (வீரமணி சர்ஃபர்)

Veeramani Surfer is a free-to-play 3D endless runner web game celebrating Tamil Nadu's vibrant street market culture (santhai). Run as Veeramani through bustling bazaar lanes, dodge auto-rickshaws and handcarts, jump over fresh vegetable crates, and slide under colorful festival awnings.

Key Features

  • High-speed 3D WebGL gameplay with smooth 60 FPS performance on mobile and desktop.
  • Authentic Tamil Nadu cultural setting with marigold flower stalls, coconut carts, and temple architecture.
  • Global and Local Online Leaderboards with instant automatic score saving.
  • Dynamic sound effects and synthesized market street ambiance.
  • Customizable runner nicknames with one-tap random name generator.
  • Full cross-device support for desktop keyboard controls, touchpads, and mobile swipe gestures.

Game Controls

Desktop: Press A or Left Arrow to move left, D or Right Arrow to move right, W, Up Arrow, or Space to jump, S or Down Arrow to slide, Esc to pause, and M to mute audio. Mobile: Swipe left or right to switch lanes, swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide, or use the on-screen touch controller buttons.

How to Play

Desktop

  • A ← move left
  • D → move right
  • W ↑ Space jump
  • S ↓ slide
  • Esc pause
  • M mute

Mobile

  • Swipe left / right — change lane
  • Swipe up — jump
  • Swipe down — slide
  • Tap the on-screen pad buttons if swiping is awkward

Rules

  • Lantern vendors sell Tamil-style tokens — grab them for points.
  • Crash once and Veeramani stumbles; crash again before he recovers and the run ends.
  • Delivery-Boy 1 and Delivery-Boy 2 close in after every stumble. Survive and they fall back.
  • Shields absorb a crash. Magnets pull tokens. Multipliers and boosts are temporary.
