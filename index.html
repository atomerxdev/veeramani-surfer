How to Play

Desktop

  • A ← move left
  • D → move right
  • W ↑ Space jump
  • S ↓ slide
  • Esc pause
  • M mute

Mobile

  • Swipe left / right — change lane
  • Swipe up — jump
  • Swipe down — slide
  • Tap the on-screen pad buttons if swiping is awkward

Rules

  • Lantern vendors sell Tamil-style tokens — grab them for points.
  • Crash once and Veeramani stumbles; crash again before he recovers and the run ends.
  • Delivery-Boy 1 and Delivery-Boy 2 close in after every stumble. Survive and they fall back.
  • Shields absorb a crash. Magnets pull tokens. Multipliers and boosts are temporary.
